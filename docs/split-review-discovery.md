# Finding additional orders to review

The default DOI worklist shows eligible and held assignments with apparent ready/waiting
patterns. A held pattern is a review lead, not permission to split. Its original split
status, rep assignment warning and other evidence holds remain visible.

Keep **Hide reviewed** selected to exclude matching completed checks. Changing the rep
scope, review group or ordering does not clear that selection or any saved check.

- **Eligible and held for verification** includes uncertain assignments for investigation;
  confirmed other-rep orders remain excluded from this default scope.
- **Eligible for my review** retains the narrower rep/currency filter.
- The default review-leads group combines existing apparent patterns and held ready/waiting
  patterns. Use the held-pattern group to focus on the latter.
- **Stock-counter clues only** is separate. A positive captured OH counter below ordered
  quantity is a reason to inspect the order, not proof of allocated or remaining units.
- **All captured orders for reconciliation** with **All split statuses** still shows the
  complete captured set subject to **Hide reviewed**.

Ready/waiting observations require distinct purchased physical lines. Components within a
kit are not separate purchase sides; a kit parent needs the existing coherent component
evidence. Structural or incomplete captures and terminal orders do not become leads merely
because an observed label looks promising.

No numeric remaining quantity, allocation or drop-ship status is inferred. Verify current
order facts, ownership, quantities, kit scope and stock before any fulfillment decision.
Full captured totals remain context rather than split value; currency conflicts remain holds.

The response change is transient. Existing reviewed checks and alternate drafts keep their
formats and evidence bindings. This isolated source change does not update a running daily
installation, activate an API or alter the separate draft-transfer workflow.
