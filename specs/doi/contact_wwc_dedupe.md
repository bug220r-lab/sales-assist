
# DOI Contact, WWC, DEDUPE, and Attention

## Contact

Contact facts are structured, source-grounded observations with direction, author/participant
classification, occurrence identity, source reference, and timing. A contact state must not be
inferred from unrestricted note text.

## WWC

WWC findings use direct `FindingPresenceV2` and closed nested schemas for identity, author
provenance, marker, timestamp, and source reference.

All legal PRESENT partitions of the governed KNOWN/UNKNOWN subfields must have executable
tests. Unknown author/time or marker facts emit their exact reason and preserve independent
known facts.

## DEDUPE

Source-observed DEDUPE findings are separate from runtime DEDUPE state/action. A PRESENT
finding must have a constructible required timestamp; an unnormalizable observed timestamp
fails before adapter success.

A DEDUPE candidate requires the complete governed conditions. A verified DEDUPE note can
reversibly close a case. No engine path writes a note or mutates an external portal.

## Attention

Recheck, intervention-verification, source-absence, and other attention conditions coexist as
cause-keyed facts. Resolving one cause does not clear unrelated causes.
