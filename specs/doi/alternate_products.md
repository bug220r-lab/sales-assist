
# DOI Alternate Product Rules

Evaluate alternate possibilities in this hierarchy without collapsing the types:

1. Exact SKU recheck — same canonical ES, improved current availability/lead.
2. Exact MFG alternate — different ES, identical canonical MFG/OE, valid relationship evidence.
3. Manufacturer cross-reference — explicit cross-reference evidence.
4. Supersession — explicit supersession evidence.
5. Functional alternate — different product may serve the function but needs fresh compatibility
   review.

Rules:

- Exact SKU is not an alternate.
- Similar descriptions never establish an alternate by themselves.
- Customer approval is required for substitutions.
- Compatibility review is required where the relationship does not independently prove fit/use.
- Relationship facts are product-only and privacy-safe.
- Relationship revisions are immutable and versioned.
- Observations bind the exact revision used.
- No verified alternate found does not prove DEDUPE eligibility.
- Manual identification blocks alternate and DEDUPE conclusions until the product/application is
  identified strongly enough.
